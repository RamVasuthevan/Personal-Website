## Keyboard shortcuts
- Browse channels: <kbd>⇧</kbd> + <kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>L</kbd>
- Start a search: <kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>G</kbd>
- Search the current conversation: <kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>F</kbd>

## Search modifiers
- `in:` `from:` `with:` — channel/DM, sender, threads and DMs with a person
- `is:saved` `has:pin` `is:thread`
- `before:` `after:` `on:` `during:`

## Notes

1. Drafts are not searchable — search only indexes sent messages, and there is no `is:draft`.
   Use **Drafts & sent** at the top of the sidebar instead.
